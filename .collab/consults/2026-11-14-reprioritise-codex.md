My independent understanding is that the owner wants **usable training and compliance documents to become the main outputs**, in this order: policies and templates; complete teaching and assessment resources; financial viability evidence. This changes work priorities, while retaining the agreed scope and director approval process.

1. **Policies, procedures and related templates.** A coordinated operating manual covering enrolment, student information and support, reasonable adjustment, assessment, RPL and credit transfer, complaints and appeals, safeguarding, workforce, facilities, records, certification, fees/refunds, governance and continuous improvement. Each procedure needs clear responsibilities, steps and usable forms/registers.

   “Pre and post validation” needs precise treatment: **Standard 1.3 requires assessment-tool review before use; Standard 1.5 requires assessment validation.** Prepare separate review and validation templates, schedules, findings and corrective-action records. Blank templates cannot establish that either activity occurred. [ASQA assessment guidance](https://www.asqa.gov.au/for-providers/standards-for-RTOs/practice-guides/quality-area-1-training-and-assessment/assessment-practice-guide)

2. **Complete resources for the agreed 12 units and standalone offerings.** Include learning materials, delivery/session plans, training and assessment strategies, student assessments, assessor instructions, marking guides with clear acceptable answers and decision rules, practical observation checklists, and mapping to every unit requirement. Include required equipment, settings, participants, repetitions/service periods, evidence records, reasonable adjustments and reassessment arrangements. Packages must suit the intended domestic 16–22 cohort and actual delivery model; mapping alone does not prove assessment quality. [ASQA assessment guidance](https://www.asqa.gov.au/for-providers/standards-for-RTOs/practice-guides/quality-area-1-training-and-assessment/assessment-practice-guide)

3. **Financial viability evidence—not just a narrative statement.** Prepare realistic revenue, expenditure, cash-flow and funding assumptions, including sites, workforce, systems, fees and delayed enrolments. ASQA’s initial-registration guide requires the **Financial Viability Risk Assessment tool**, business plan, bank statements and a nominated accountant’s certificate signed within **30 days of submission**. AI can prepare working material; genuine financial evidence and accountant involvement remain necessary. [ASQA initial-registration guide](https://www.asqa.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-03/Guide%20to%20initial%20RTO%20registration.pdf)
