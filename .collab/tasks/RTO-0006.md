**Queensland screening and minors**

The RTO exclusion must be assessed separately for each activity:

- **Ordinary RTO teaching:** excluded from the private-teaching category. [Guidance—opened today](https://www.qld.gov.au/jobs/blue-card/required/individuals-businesses/private-teaching-coaching-tutoring).
- **Education programs outside school:** the category guidance also expressly excludes RTO employees/businesses; approved flexible-learning involvement does not automatically remove that exclusion. [Guidance—opened today](https://www.qld.gov.au/jobs/blue-card/required/individuals-businesses/education-programs-conducted-outside-school).
- **Services delivered inside schools:** school-based work/services while children are present can trigger the school categories, subject to applicable exemptions. [Guidance—opened today](https://www.qld.gov.au/jobs/blue-card/required/individuals-businesses/schools).
- **Counselling, emotional support, mentoring or pastoral care:** separately assess actual duties, including online services. Do not assume every learning-support interaction qualifies, or that the teaching exclusion covers these services. [Guidance—opened today](https://www.qld.gov.au/jobs/blue-card/required/individuals-businesses/health-counselling-support-services).
- **Directors and placements:** executive-officer requirements can follow an applicable regulated business category. Assess actual placement roles/settings rather than screening every hospitality supervisor merely because a learner is 16–17. [Category overview—opened today](https://www.qld.gov.au/jobs/blue-card/required/individuals-businesses).

For licensed-premises practicals, Queensland guidance permits under-18s to serve/supply alcohol in hotels, restaurants and cafés without adult-entertainment permits. This does not confer unrestricted premises access. [RSA guidance—opened today](https://www.business.qld.gov.au/industries/hospitality-tourism-sport/liquor-gaming/liquor/training/rsa/certification).
