- **R2-01 Schedule and status (HIGH).** The October outputs are unevidenced, and the plan's dates have expired. Record actual status and adopt a recovery sequence (section 6).
- **R2-02 Practical settings (Claude: CRITICAL; Codex: HIGH; see section 9).** The owner's plan for practicals (home, own workplace, or in-house office) has not been shown to work unit by unit. - **R2-04 Checkpoint criteria (HIGH).** The 28 Feb, 15 Mar and 25 Mar gates have no written pass/fail criteria or blockers. Include "no lodgement without company and accountant". Include workflow tests for enrolment, support, assessment, complaints and appeals, certification and reporting, and evidence retrieval, recording failures and retests.
- **R2-05 Specialists (HIGH).** There is no tracked scope, engagement, availability, turnaround, cost or reopening rule for specialist review. Get the pilot specialist-reviewed before it is copied to the other units.
- **R2-06 Owner decisions not applied (HIGH).** See section 5.
- **R2-07 Review workflow (MEDIUM).** The plan names Claude alone as reviewer. Record the independent Claude and Codex reviews, reconciliation, ChatGPT's disposition, and escalation after round 2.
- **R2-08 Document and bundle controls (MEDIUM).** Define changes during a review and the downstream impact. Specify the minimum controls for P01–P05.
- **R2-09 RMLV trainer credential (HIGH).** See section 2. A director action: ask OLGR in writing.
- **R2-10 Safeguarding and minors (HIGH).** The role-by-role matrix (APR-01/P11) is still queued. Points found:
  - A minor on licensed premises to work, or receiving training for employment or work experience, is an "exempt minor" under the Liquor Act 1992. The after-5pm nightclub limit applies only to the separate meal/accompanying-adult exemption. Adult-entertainment venues need their own check.
  - Minors may not play gaming machines (Gaming Machine Act ss253–254). Use a lawful simulation for SITHGAM022.
  - Work-experience placements need written arrangements, principal approval and insurance (Education (Work Experience) Act 1996, ss6–9). Parental consent is also needed for minors.
  - A learner Blue Card is needed only where a placement involves a regulated child-related activity.
- **R2-11 Source capture (MEDIUM).** Add the captured sources to SRC-01. Capture each unit's current Assessment Requirements before authoring that unit.
- **New, LOW: competing demand on director time.** Two Website Development documents (19 Oct) plan a separate non-accredited awareness-course business. They correctly exclude RTO and "nationally recognised" wording. That work draws on the same director time as this project, so the reforecast should account for it.
