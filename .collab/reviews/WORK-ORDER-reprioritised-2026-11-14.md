## 3. What every unit package must contain

- **Learner material** for the 16–22 domestic cohort: plain English, online-first, with accessibility and LLN/digital support built in.
- **Assessment tasks** that collect every item of performance evidence (including the required number of occasions and service periods) and every item of knowledge evidence.
- **Marking guide** with model answers or benchmarks and clear satisfactory/not-yet-satisfactory decision rules.
- **Observation checklists** for practical tasks. Each lists the observable behaviours, the conditions, the equipment and the participants the unit specifies.
- **Assessor guide:** instructions, conditions, reasonable adjustment, reassessment and authenticity checks.
- **Mapping** of every element, performance criterion, foundation skill, performance evidence item, knowledge evidence item and assessment condition to the specific task and question.
- **Delivery and session plan,** and a **resource and equipment list**.
- **Practical setting statement** for each task: online or virtual if the unit allows it, otherwise the office simulated environment. For the office, list exactly what the simulation must contain. This becomes the input for costing the office setup. For example:
  - **SITXFSA006:** an operational food preparation area with a commercial-grade bench, refrigeration, a temperature probe and handwashing facilities.
  - **SITHGAM022:** a simulated gaming environment with industry-realistic gambling activities. Minors may not play gaming machines.
  - **SITHIND007:** 12 complete service periods in an operational hospitality environment, with an industry-realistic staff-to-customer ratio.
- **Source record:** the unit's current release and its Assessment Requirements with the retrieval date. Capture these from training.gov.au before writing.

## 4. What every policy bundle must contain

Purpose and scope; clause references to the 2025 Standards; roles and responsibilities; procedure steps; the forms, registers and templates that put it into practice; records kept and how long; review cycle; version control. Where something depends on an arrangement that doesn't exist yet (entity, staff, Ammonite), write it as a marked assumption. Never imply the arrangement exists.

## 5. Financial viability: what ASQA requires

ASQA's Guide to initial RTO registration (v1.6) requires:
- ASQA's **Financial Viability Risk Assessment tool**, with forecast financial information for the next 12 and 24 months;
- a **business plan**;
- **bank statements**;
- a **nominated accountant's certificate signed within 30 days of submission**.

The assessment must be done before the application is submitted, by the applicant together with a nominated accountant. That accountant must be independent of the applicant, with no financial stake, and a member of CPA Australia, CA ANZ or the IPA, or an ASIC-registered auditor.

The AI can draft the business plan, the forecast structure and the assumptions. The real figures, the entity, the bank records and the accountant's certificate must be genuine. They depend on the owner's instruction that there is no lodgement without the company and accountant in place.

Source: [ASQA Guide to initial RTO registration v1.6](https://www.asqa.gov.au/sites/default/files/2026-03/Guide%20to%20initial%20RTO%20registration.pdf) (checked 14 Nov 2026).

## 6. How drafts come to review

1. Save each draft as a new, versioned Google Doc in the right folder (02 Policies and Procedures, 03 Training and Assessment Strategies, 04 Learning and Assessment Resources, or 06 Application Evidence for financial items). Set REG-01 to READY FOR AI REVIEW.
2. Send drafts in batches: up to 5 policy bundles, or 1–2 unit packages. Write a short handover listing the IDs, versions, links and any open questions.
3. The directors bring the batch to Claude. Claude copies it into the review repository. Claude and Codex then review independently, reconcile, and save one review document per batch in 00 Project Controls.
4. ChatGPT responds to each finding and revises. A second review round follows if needed. Unresolved material issues go to the directors.
5. Only the directors approve documents. A specialist-pending document cannot be used for delivery.

## 7. What Claude and Codex will check

- Accuracy against the current official sources: the unit's release and Assessment Requirements, and the 2025 Standards.
- **Assessment quality,** not just completed mapping cells. Every performance and knowledge evidence item must be genuinely assessed, the conditions met, the decision rules clear and the evidence observable.
- That the setting assumptions follow the practical setting rule, and that the office simulation list is complete.
- That policies are workable for an RTO of this size, with roles, steps, forms and records consistent across documents.
- No invented facts, staff, consultation, facilities, figures or approvals.
- That scope, electives and Ammonite are unchanged and no API or n8n spend is introduced.
